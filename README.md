# Meme Deck

[Open the soundboard](https://chatgptricks.github.io/meme-deck-pages/)

128 pads in one classic Braun-inspired layout: 112 real audio clips with offline support and 16 online Betty la Fea music cues. Warm ivory, clear controls and tactile pads fill the screen on iPhone and desktop.

On iPhone, open the site in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. Open once online and wait for OFFLINE READY to save the 112 bundled clips. Betty cues play through YouTube embeds for up to about one minute, require an internet connection and depend on source availability; their media is not bundled or cached.

This repository contains only the published app. Attribution is available in the sound library, sounds.json and betty.json. Recordings belong to their respective rights holders.
